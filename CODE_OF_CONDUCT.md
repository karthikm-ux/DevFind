# DevFind Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors and maintainers of DevFind, we are committed to providing an open, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, race, ethnicity, or religion.

## Expected Standards

We encourage behaviors that foster a positive collaborative community:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
- Providing constructive, thoughtful feedback on code, issues, and pull requests.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Maintaining security awareness—never sharing live credentials, tokens, or sensitive production data in public discussions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Harassment, trolling, offensive commentary, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit consent.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or unwelcome attention.
- Any conduct reasonably deemed inappropriate in a professional open-source environment.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards. If you observe or experience unacceptable behavior, please reach out to the project maintainer at `aadiur710@gmail.com`. All reports will be handled promptly, discreetly, and fairly.
